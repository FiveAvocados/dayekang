Tomorrow

‘Tomorrow’ is a service designed to teach coding and French to refugee children in France through French children's books. After receiving a smart device from a donor, children learn French and coding simultaneously through French children's books. Using block-based coding methods, children can easily make interactive storybooks.

What inspired this project

While I was traveling in Paris, I witnessed conflicts between locals and refugees. There were police officers at the museums and the Eiffel Tower. I wondered whether technology could help ease these conflicts. After some research, I found that many refugees had difficulty adjusting to French society because of the language. Many of them also did not have a chance to attend school. Meanwhile, Europe needed more IT experts. I wanted to help refugee children through an app that they could use to learn French and coding together.

Service Summary

Reality: In France, many refugees cannot adjust to society, so social conflicts are widespread. This creates tension in society.

Problem: Refugee children in France do not have enough educational opportunities, and because they cannot speak French, they cannot socialize with their peers and may feel alienated. In addition, they may have trauma from the war.

Solution: Refugee children can use French children's books to learn French, which can help them adjust to society. Because classic children's books are no longer under copyright, children can use them for free. With help and donations from French citizens, children can receive French audiobooks recorded by citizens and donated cellphones for studying. They can enjoy the learning process while making the books interactive.

Importance: The service can contribute to a more harmonious society and support the education of refugee children. IT education can also help address Europe's workforce shortage.

Design

The service has two connected versions: one for French citizens who donate unused devices or record stories, and one for refugee children who learn French and coding through those stories.

French citizen

Donor

  1. Donate unused smart devices to refugee children.
  2. Record a French audiobook for a child to listen to.
Learner

Refugee child

  1. Read a children's book while learning French and coding.
  2. Unlock and read more interactive books.
  3. Join learning events created for children.
Learning interface

Reading Page

  1. Study French through dialogue in the book.
  2. Learn coding with simple block-based coding methods.
  3. Fill in the blank with the correct French word.
  4. See the Little Prince react, then continue the story.
