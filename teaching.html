Cornell University Spring 2025, 2026

Design AI Products and Services

Course Number
INFO 4940 / INFO 5940
Role
As a teaching assistant, I contributed to the development of stakeholder-mapping and role-play activities about AI limitations and harms, facilitated class discussions, and met with teams weekly to critique the sociotechnical context of their projects.
Course Length
15 Weeks
Course Description
This course prepares students to design AI products and services that maximize societal benefit while minimizing risk and harm. Through hands-on projects and discussion, students examine the sociotechnical ecosystems surrounding AI systems, apply principles of effective human–AI interaction—including collaboration, explainability, trust, and fairness—and work in interdisciplinary teams to create systems that empower users, benefit society, and resist bias and manipulation.

Human-Computer Interaction Design

Course Number
INFO 3450 / COMM 3450 / INFO 5355
Role
As a teaching assistant, I provided in-class studio support and weekly written feedback across user research, prototyping, evaluation, and final demos, formed project teams, and mentored students on collaboration, portfolios, and career decisions.
Course Length
14 Weeks
Course Description
This course introduces human-centered design as a process for creating digital products that work well for people. Students learn to conduct user research and define user needs, explore and justify design solutions, build and iterate interactive prototypes, evaluate usability and usefulness, and collaborate effectively in team-based design projects.

Cornell University Fall 2023, 2024

Visual Data Analytics for the Web

Course Number
INFO 3300 / INFO 5100 / CS 3300
Role
As a teaching assistant, I advised students on visualization concepts and D3.js implementation, helping them work through technical barriers with guided questions and peer collaboration that strengthened independent problem-solving skills.
Course Length
15 Weeks
Course Description
This course is an introduction to designing and building interactive, web-based data visualizations with JavaScript, D3.js, and SVG. Students learn to gather and model real-world data, match data with effective visual forms, and apply visualization theory and critique to create clear dynamic visualizations.
