Interactive Data Visualization

INFO 3300 / INFO 5100 / CS 3300 · Teaching Assistant

Selected outcomes from Project 2, where students designed interactive visual explanations with D3.js. I supported the course as a teaching assistant through technical guidance, critique, and project feedback.

Student work

01

NBA Legends: Career Highlights and Earnings

Dylan Aquart, Ebenezer Eshetu, Tejhan Diallo, and Yvon Gatete

An interactive visualization for comparing Michael Jordan, Kobe Bryant, and LeBron James across seasons using user-selected performance and salary metrics. Hovering over a point reveals the corresponding season and player details, making standout seasons and statistical outliers easy to explore.

02

Netflix and Disney+: A Catalog Comparison

Samarth Chitgopekar, Amad Kabir, Daniel Parker, and Aaishi Uppuluri

An interactive comparison of Netflix and Disney+ catalogs across genre distribution, country representation, and releases over time. The coordinated charts help viewers assess which service better matches their interests.

03

Global GDP, Inequality, and Emissions

John Guo, Luke Murphy, Amy Li, and Jenny Chen

An interactive world map connects GDP, Gini inequality, and CO₂ emissions from 1990 to 2022. Viewers can change the year and indicator, then select a country to examine its economic, social, and environmental trends in detail.

Open interactive visualization ↗
04

Cost of Living Across the United States

Carly Hu, Myna Lim, Tasnimul Taher, and Sydney Wan

A choropleth and linked charts compare annual living costs across U.S. states by expense category and family size. Filters reveal geographic patterns and how housing, food, transportation, healthcare, childcare, and taxes change across household configurations.

Open interactive visualization ↗
05

Global Natural Disaster Mortality

Stella Han, Jennie Zhang, Jane Gao, and Kyung Min Lee

An interactive map traces deaths from natural disasters across countries and disaster types from 1900 to 2020. A companion line chart compares broader causes of death from 2000 to 2019, helping viewers examine distinct and overlapping mortality trends.
