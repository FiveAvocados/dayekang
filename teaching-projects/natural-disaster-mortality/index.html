Global Natural Disaster Mortality: A Century-Long Global Trend

This interactive map shows the trend in the number of deaths caused by natural disasters across the world between
the years 1900 and 2020. The varying hues represent varying numbers of death. The filter can show the deaths caused by a
specific natural disaster, while the time slider can show the change in the number of deaths in a particular year.

Mortality Trends: 100 Years of Heart Disease, Cancer, Accidents, Suicide, Earthquakes, Hurricanes

This interactive line graph shows the relationship between the trends in the number of deaths caused by the different
factors across the world between the years 2000 and 2019. The numbers labeled when the graph is hovered over is the
number of deaths caused in that year.
